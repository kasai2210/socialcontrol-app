Support

SocialControl for iOS & Android · Updated 2026-11-26

Contact us

Email bhoopen22oct@gmail.com with your device model, OS version (iOS or Android), app version, and which space or control you were using. Please do not send passwords, session cookies, private messages, or account recovery codes.

If a website layout looks broken

Social websites change their markup frequently. Inside any space, open the More (…) menu and choose Report filter issue. You can temporarily pause cosmetic CSS for the current visit to verify whether a filter caused the glitch, and copy the non-sensitive diagnostic summary to email us.

Sign-in, playback, and cache tips

If a space feels stuck or fails to load, try reloading the page, toggling the relevant filter, or opening Settings → Clear website cache (keeps sign-ins). Website sign-in methods, uploads, Picture-in-Picture, and background playback depend on your OS, the website, your account, and regional rules. SocialControl does not bypass paid features or regional restrictions.

Local data and accounts

In Settings, you can clear website data for an individual space or erase all local SocialControl data, preferences, timers, and schedules. Clearing local data does not delete your third-party social account; manage or delete third-party accounts through each service's own settings.

App Shield (Android) & Screen Time (iOS)

Both are strictly optional. On Android, App Shield uses Android Accessibility settings solely to notice which chosen app comes to the front; you can disable it anytime in Android Settings → Accessibility. On iOS (Screen Time build), you can revoke Screen Time authorization anytime in iOS Settings → Screen Time.
